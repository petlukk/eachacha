Manually inline all rotl16/rotl12/rotl8/rotl7 function calls in the 4-block ILP decrypt loop to eliminate function call overhead. With 320 rotl calls per 256B chunk (32 per double-round × 10 rounds), even 5 cycles of call/return overhead per invocation adds ~1600 cycles per chunk — matching the observed gap between 0.5 GB/s actual and ~1.4 GB/s theoretical. The Ea compiler at opt-level=3 may not inline these helper functions.
